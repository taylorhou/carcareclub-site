Why one area first

A membership is only worth it if there's a car wash in the network near you. So we open one area at a time, and Houston is first.

Membership opens in an area once enough stations there have signed on. Everyone on that area's waitlist hears first.

How it works

Three steps, once your area opens

  1. Sign up

    Join online, pick a plan and pay monthly. No app to download.

  2. Pull into a station in the network

    Look for the Car Care Club sticker at the wash bay.

  3. Scan and wash

    Scan the sticker with your phone, then wash.

Price

Questions

Common questions

When does membership open in Houston?

When enough Houston stations have joined. We don't have a date yet. Everyone on the Houston waitlist hears first.

What happens when I join the waitlist?

You send us a short email with your zip code. We add you to the list for your area and email you when it opens. There's nothing to pay now.

I'm not in Houston. Can I join?

Yes. Tell us your city and zip code and we'll add you to the list for your area. It helps us decide where to go next.

Do I need an app?

No. You scan the sticker at the wash bay with your phone.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. No contract and no cancellation fees.

Car Care Club is a startup and we're building this now. If something here doesn't make sense, email team@carcareclub.co.
