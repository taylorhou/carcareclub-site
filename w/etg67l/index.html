How it works

Three steps for your station

  1. We mail you a QR sticker

    You put it up at your wash bay.

  2. A member scans it at the bay

    They scan it with their phone and wash.

  3. We pay you $3-6 per wash

    Once a month, by direct deposit. The more a member washes in a month, the less per wash, but it always pays you.

Questions

What owners ask us

Who are the members?

People who pay Car Care Club a monthly fee to wash their car at gas station car washes in our network. Once you join, your wash is one of them.

How is a wash verified?

The member scans the QR sticker at your bay with their phone. The scan records the wash with us, and each recorded wash is a payout to you.

What if the bay is busy?

Members wait their turn like any other customer. Nothing changes about how you run the wash.

How and when do I get paid?

By direct deposit, once a month, for the month before, with a statement listing every member wash. You get $3-6 for each wash. Each member pays us monthly, and that fee is shared across the washes they do that month, so one or two washes pay you $6 each and a member who washes often pays a little less per wash, never under $3.

Can I stop?

Yes, any time. It's month to month with no fees. Tell us and we take your station out of the network.

Where are you live?

Texas and Connecticut first. We're adding stations there now.
